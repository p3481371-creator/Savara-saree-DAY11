Caring for silk

So it lasts a lifetime

Read the full guide

Wrap in muslin

Never plastic. Muslin lets silk breathe and keeps folds from yellowing.

Refold each season

Change where the creases fall every three months so the zari never cracks.

Air in shade

An hour in shade after wearing. Direct sun fades arakku and peacock green.

Dry clean first

Always for Kanchipuram and tissue. Silk cotton can be hand washed after its first clean.
