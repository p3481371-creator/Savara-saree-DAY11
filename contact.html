Questions

Before you ask

Anything not here, write to us and we'll answer within a day.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes, to most countries. Sarees ship insured by courier in their muslin wrap and arrive in 6 to 10 working days. Duties are charged by your country on arrival.

How do I know the silk and zari are pure?

Every silk saree carries a Silk Mark tag from the Silk Mark Organisation of India. The weaver's card inside lists the zari used, and you are welcome to test a thread in the store.

Can I return a saree?

Ready sarees can be returned unworn with their tags within 10 days. Sarees woven to order, including bridal borders drawn for you, cannot be returned.

How long does a custom saree take?

About six weeks for a standard Kanchipuram and twelve weeks for a bridal saree, from colour selection to delivery. Our rush loom can shorten a bridal order to about 18 days of weaving.

Can I watch my saree being woven?

Yes. The looms are below the store and open to visitors from 10 am to 5 pm. Bridal customers receive photographs from the loom each week.

Do you stitch blouses?

Yes, from the saree's own blouse piece, with two fittings. Blouse tailoring is included with every bridal saree.
